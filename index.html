Return Policy

We want you to be happy with your order. You can request a return within 7 days of receiving your product.

  • The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags and accessories.
  • Contact us on WhatsApp (0327 340 8820) with your order number and a photo of the product.
  • If an item arrives damaged or wrong, tell us as soon as you receive it and we will arrange a replacement or refund.
  • Approved refunds are returned using the same method you paid with, or arranged with you for Cash on Delivery orders.

Privacy Policy

NextBuy.pk collects only the details needed to run your order and account: name, email, phone number, delivery address and order history.

  • We use your details to process and deliver orders, contact you about them, and help with support.
  • Passwords are stored in a hashed form and are never visible to us.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We share only what delivery and payment partners need to complete your order.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your account details by contacting us on WhatsApp.

Terms of Service

  • Prices are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) and may change without notice. Your order price is the one shown at checkout.
  • Orders are confirmed after we contact you. We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock or the details are incomplete.
  • Delivery is free across Pakistan. Delivery times depend on your city and courier.
  • Returns follow our Return Policy.
  • By placing an order you confirm that the details you provided are correct.
